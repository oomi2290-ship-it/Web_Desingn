About our museum

A student-friendly place to learn about the past.

Our beginning

Started by a small community

Old Stones Museum began when local families donated objects they no longer used. They wanted younger people to see how daily life looked before modern technology.

Today, volunteers take care of the objects and explain their stories to visitors. We keep the descriptions simple because history should be open to everyone.

Our purpose
